Personal Information

About Me

I'm Pridhvi Koushi, a third-year Electronics and Communication Engineering (ECE) student and an Overall Coordinator at the E-Cell. I bridge technical execution with strategic ecosystem building. I am committed to mentoring team leads, fostering high-value collaborations with external incubators, and delivering transformative experiences that turn aspiring student minds into confident startup founders.

Motivation

Why I Joined E-Cell

I joined E-Cell to foster a culture where student innovation moves beyond classrooms and into the market. Experiencing the startup ecosystem firsthand inspired me to lead E-Cell in building high-impact programs, strong industry networks, and an empowering environment for the next generation of founders.

Motivation

My motivation stems from witnessing bright engineering ideas remain confined to classroom projects. Having experienced the journey of turning technical research into a working MVP with a real world problem, I am driven to remove the barriers student founders face. I am motivated by the challenge of building structured platforms, opening national doors, and watching early-stage student concepts evolve into scalable, high-impact startups.

Direction

Vision

To position our institution as a premier hub for student-led entrepreneurship — where technical innovation seamlessly transforms into scalable, real-world startups that solve critical technological and environmental challenges.

Execution

Mission

  • Organize meaningful entrepreneurship-focused events and workshops.
  • Promote E-Cell activities effectively across the campus.
  • Strengthen communication between E-Cell and students.
  • Direct cross-functional teams (Tech, PR, Events, Corporate Relations) to plan and deliver high-value entrepreneurship summits, bootcamps, and workshops.
  • Build active networks and partnerships with industry experts, startup incubators, venture funds, and external organizations.
  • Strategically elevate E-Cell's presence across campus to maximize student participation and build an active community of student entrepreneurs.
Selection

Why I Was Selected for This Portfolio

I was selected as Overall Coordinator because of my combination of hands-on startup experience, technical depth, and proven team leadership. I bring practical insights into venture building, pitch strategy, and project execution. My strength lies in uniting cross-functional teams, building strategic industry connections, and executing high-impact initiatives that elevate our campus entrepreneurial ecosystem.
